About this tool

The Google Play Screenshot Validator was built to save Android developers a round trip to the Play Console. Instead of uploading a screenshot only to find out it's the wrong size, you can check dimensions, aspect ratio and file format instantly, in the browser, before you ever open your store listing.

Why it works this way

Everything — validation and the auto-fix (padding or cropping to a safe size) — runs locally using the browser's File and Canvas APIs. Nothing is uploaded to a server, so it's fast and it works with confidential or unreleased app screenshots.

Who it's for

Indie developers, app store optimization teams, and QA reviewers preparing a batch of screenshots for a Play Store listing.
