Google Play screenshot dimensions

Google Play accepts phone screenshots within a broad technical range. For recommendation-friendly phone screenshots, 1080 × 1920 portrait and 1920 × 1080 landscape are common targets.

Common screenshot mistakes

Too small

A side below 320 px can fail the technical requirement.

Too large

A side above 3840 px exceeds the supported dimension range.

Ratio too extreme

The longest side must not be more than twice the shortest side.

Wrong file format

Use JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha for screenshots.

FAQ

What is the best Google Play screenshot size?

1080 × 1920 portrait and 1920 × 1080 landscape are widely used targets for high-quality phone screenshots.

What is the minimum screenshot size?

The minimum allowed dimension is 320 px.

Can I upload 1440 × 2560 screenshots?

Yes, if the image remains within Google Play's dimension and aspect-ratio requirements.
