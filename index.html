Common Screenshot Errors

Aspect ratio over 2:1

Full-page scrolling captures or screenshots with extra chrome stitched on often end up far taller than they are wide, pushing past Google's 2:1 limit.

Dimension below 320px

Thumbnails or resized previews exported instead of the original capture usually fail the 320px minimum on their shortest side.

Dimension above 3840px

Raw captures from very high-resolution or multi-monitor setups can exceed the 3840px maximum on their longest side.

Wrong file format

WEBP, GIF, HEIC and other formats aren't accepted — only JPG and 24-bit PNG are.

Inconsistent set sizes

Mixing portrait and landscape, or different resolutions, across one screenshot set looks unpolished even when each image individually passes.

Heavy, uncompressed files

Uncompressed exports can quietly exceed Google Play's recommended 8MB per screenshot, slowing down your listing upload.

FAQ

What is the correct Google Play screenshot size?

Anywhere from 320px to 3840px per side is technically valid, as long as the longest side is no more than twice the shortest. For a clean, high-resolution listing, Google recommends 1080×1920 for portrait screenshots and 1920×1080 for landscape.

What's the difference between Play Store screenshot size and Google Play screenshot requirements?

They describe the same thing from two angles: "screenshot size" refers to the pixel dimensions and ratio, while "requirements" also cover accepted file formats (JPG/PNG) and how many screenshots your listing needs.

Why did my screenshot fail validation?

Usually one of four reasons: the aspect ratio exceeds 2:1, a side is under 320px, a side is over 3840px, or the file isn't JPG/PNG. This tool names the exact reason under each result.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. Validation and fixing both run locally using your browser's File and Canvas APIs. Your screenshots never leave your device.

What file formats does Google Play accept?

JPEG (JPG) and 24-bit PNG. This validator only accepts those two formats as well, matching Google Play's own rules.
