Google Play app icon requirements

The Play Store listing icon has a fixed technical specification. It is separate from your Android launcher icon and is uploaded as a higher-resolution store asset.

Dimensions
512 × 512 px
Format
32-bit PNG

Store icon vs launcher icon

Your Google Play store icon does not replace the icon packaged inside your Android app. Google Play uses the store-listing icon in places such as the listing, search results and charts.

FAQ

What size should a Google Play app icon be?

512 × 512 pixels.

Can a Google Play app icon have transparency?

Yes. Google specifies a 32-bit PNG with alpha for the store-listing app icon.

What is the maximum app icon file size?

1024 KB.
